About Undersigned

Undersigned is a lightweight app for community advocacy that helps people send individual letters about a shared concern.

It began with a question: What is the smallest amount of technology required to make community action possible?

The idea came from a group of seniors who wanted to speak up about changes in their community. Some were comfortable with technology; others struggled with the mechanics of copying and pasting text, adding email addresses, and sending messages to multiple recipients. I wanted to make participation easier without asking anyone to learn another tool.

How it works

One person writes a letter, adds the email addresses of the people who need to receive it, and shares a link. Everyone who opens the link can send the same letter to the same recipients from their own email, with the option to personalize it before sending.

The person who creates the link does the heavy lifting. Everyone else just needs to click a button.

Built to do less

No account to create. No text or addresses to copy and paste. No petition to sign when what you want to do is send a letter.

Each message comes directly from the person sending it, allowing a group to communicate a shared position without losing the individual voices behind it. And because the letter travels in the link, there’s no need to store letter content on a server.

I collect only limited usage analytics to understand how the app is being used. The letter itself isn’t collected through those analytics.

Who is it for?

Undersigned began with seniors, but the idea applies anywhere people want to speak up together: residents responding to a proposed neighbourhood change, parents contacting a school board, community members writing to elected representatives, or members of an organization contacting its board.

These groups don’t necessarily need a campaign platform. They need a straightforward way to help people take the same action.

Undersigned turns shared concern into individual action.
